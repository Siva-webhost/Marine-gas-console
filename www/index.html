💨Calibration Gas Flow Rate Converter
ℹ️
NL = Normal Litre (0 °C, 101.325 kPa). SL = Standard Litre (20 °C, 101.325 kPa, ISO). Actual L/min varies with temperature; NL/SL are referenced to a fixed temperature. NL/min = L/min × 273.15 ÷ (273.15 + T). SL/min = L/min × 293.15 ÷ (273.15 + T).

✅
Typical Calibration Flow Rates: Portable aspirated 0.5–1.0 L/min · Fixed detector 0.5–2.0 L/min · PID 0.3–0.5 L/min · Diffusion instruments need no external flow. Always apply gas at slightly higher flow than sensor demand for a stable reading.
